# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who need to edit PDFs without friction, especially students and professionals. They may be fixing a form, annotating notes, adjusting a document, adding text or signatures, cleaning up a page, or exporting a finished PDF quickly. They expect the document to render exactly as uploaded and do not want to learn a complex publishing tool.

## Product Purpose

A local-first PDF editor that lets users import a PDF, make practical edits, and export the edited file with minimal friction. Success means a user can open a PDF, complete a useful edit, and download the result without account pressure, clutter, upload anxiety, or paid-tool complexity.

## Brand Personality

Clean, fast, trustworthy. The product should feel calm, precise, and dependable, with the restraint of a focused professional tool. It should earn confidence by staying out of the way.

## Anti-references

Avoid Adobe-level complexity, cluttered multi-row toolbars, messy or noisy online PDF tools, paywall-heavy SaaS patterns, childish visuals, and anything that feels like a random upload site. The target feel is closer to a minimal Linear-like editor: structured, quiet, sharp, and task-first.

## Design Principles

1. Render first, edit second: the PDF itself is the main artifact, and the interface should protect visual fidelity.
2. First value in one minute: import, edit, export should be obvious without onboarding ceremony.
3. Tools stay quiet until needed: expose the core actions immediately and move advanced controls into contextual panels.
4. Local-first trust: communicate privacy through behavior and copy, not fear-based banners.
5. Familiar beats clever: use standard editing affordances, visible selection states, predictable keyboard shortcuts, and conventional file actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Support keyboard-friendly tool access, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, color-blind-safe states, reduced-motion preferences, large hit targets for editing handles, and clear non-color indicators for selected or active tools.
